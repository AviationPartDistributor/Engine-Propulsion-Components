## Aircraft Compressor Components

Compressor sections increase air pressure before the air enters the combustion system. Components can include compressor blades, vanes, rotors, stators, seals, spacers, and associated hardware.

Maintenance and overhaul programs may require individual compressor components or complete assemblies according to the applicable engine configuration.

## Aircraft Turbine Components

Turbine sections extract energy from hot combustion gases to drive engine components. Turbine parts can include blades, vanes, discs, seals, shrouds, and attachment hardware.

## Aircraft Engine Bearings and Seals

Bearings support rotating assemblies while seals help control fluid and air movement within engine systems.

These components are critical to engine operation and may be replaced during inspection, repair, or overhaul activities. Proper part identification is essential for procurement.

## Aircraft Engine Mounting Components

## Aircraft Engine Exhaust Components

Exhaust components manage the flow of gases leaving the engine. Depending on the propulsion system, these may include exhaust cones, ducts, nozzles, seals, clamps, brackets, and related hardware.

Maintenance teams may require replacement exhaust components following inspections or scheduled engine maintenance.
